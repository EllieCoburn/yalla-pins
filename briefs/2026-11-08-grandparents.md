## 2. Babyproofing before a visit (no-purchase)
- Search: babyproofing grandparents house
- Graphic: eyebrow "For grandparents"; headline "Before they visit, / *get down low.*"; list: Medicines and purses up high · Cords and blind strings tucked away · Cleaning supplies off low shelves · Stairs blocked off · One low drawer they can empty
- Title: Babyproofing Grandparents' House: A Quick Checklist Before the Grandkids Visit
- Description: Before a toddler visits, sit on the floor in each room and look at what's at their height. Move medicines, purses and cleaning supplies up high. Tuck away cords and blind strings, block the stairs, and push breakables back from table edges. Then leave one low drawer of safe things they're allowed to empty.
